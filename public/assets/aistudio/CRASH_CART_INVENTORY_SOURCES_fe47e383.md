# Crash-cart inventory sources

The CODE 100 training trolley is a combined educational inventory. The six-drawer arrangement follows the Government of India emergency-care resuscitation-trolley checklist; additional airway and circulation equipment is included from the Oman Ministry of Health crash-cart guideline. The two source documents use different drawer configurations, so this application does not claim to reproduce a single universal cart layout.

1. **Government of India, Ministry of Health & Family Welfare / NHSRC.** *Operational and Technical Guidelines on Emergency Care Services at District Hospital*, Annexure VII, “Emergency Resuscitation Trolley.” The source lists top-shelf equipment and six drawers covering emergency medicines, suction supplies, IV access and disposables, airway equipment, and fluids.
   - https://nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/OT_emergency%20services%20at%20DH_inside_20-03-2023.pdf
2. **Oman Ministry of Health, Directorate General of Private Health Establishments.** *Emergency Crash Cart & Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation Guidelines*, October 2023, Annex One. The source describes a four-drawer configuration, top-shelf equipment, medication stock, airway equipment, IV-access supplies, fluids and resuscitation equipment. It states that the cart should be checked daily and that paediatric sizes should be included where the facility provides paediatric services.
   - https://www.moh.gov.om/media/qejghazu/guidelines-for-emergency-crash-cart-cpr.pdf

## Important implementation limits

- The inventory labels and example quantities are for simulation and must be reconciled with the facility's approved crash-cart list. Stock, concentrations, formulations, drawer order and paediatric equipment vary by institution.
- Drug names and strengths are labels only. The game intentionally does not calculate or recommend medication doses.
- Taking or using an item updates the shared training inventory, team chat and instructor report queue. It does not administer a real medicine or automatically alter patient physiology.
- Before formal training, an instructor should verify all medications, expiry dates, equipment checks, quantities and age-appropriate sizes against current local policy.
